      About fridgecook

      Tick what you have and fridgecook shows dishes you can cook right now, plus those that need one or two more things. It also tells you which single item would unlock the most dishes.

      About a hundred Indian and everyday dishes, each with short steps. Filters for veg, veg with eggs, and Jain (no onion, garlic or root vegetables).

      What you tick is remembered on this device, so next time you only change what's new.
